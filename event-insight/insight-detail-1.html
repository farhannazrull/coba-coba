Kick Off Danamon Innovation Race 2023: Better, Faster, and Stronger

Jessica Jasmine Pratiwi Sumarsono

8 Juli 2023

Read 8 Min

Bank Danamon held the Danamon Innovation Race (DIR). The event was to accommodate employees to convey ideas, innovations and implementations.


The event is nuanced competence among employees, which is also a management support to explore and express the potential of its employees.


Danamon's President Director, Daisuke Ejima, said that his party conducted a series of activities, from training, internal and external mentoring, progress checkpoints, and a final presentation involving senior management to the awarding ceremony.


"This year's DIR awarding ceremony was themed 'Better Faster Stronger' and was attended by management, work unit leaders, mentors, facilitators, and employees as both participants and winners of the competition. During the six months of competition, the participants were equipped with various relevant training, including design thinking (innovation), project management, risk & governance, and presentation skills," he said in his written statement on Monday (12/25/2023).


In addition, the participants were also provided with mentoring sessions with internal mentors and external facilitators until the project was finally executed and implemented properly.


"My message to all employees is to never stop innovating and bringing changes that can support business growth and company performance going forward," he said.


Danamon's Chief Human Capital, Evi Damayanti, advised more employees to participate in the Danamon Innovation Race and collaborate with other work units.
