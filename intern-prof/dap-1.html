Questions Related to the Internship Program

  • DAP is a Kampus Merdeka learning program (training, leadership camp, hands-on projects, & daily tasks), and Regular Internship is a learning experience in a division including hands-on projects (according to the division's needs) and daily tasks.

  • The DAP training will be taught about bank-wide learning, management, and leadership.

  • The training is held at Danamon Corporate University, Ciawi.

  • Yes, program participants will receive an allowance according to applicable regulations.

  • Yes, interns can work in all Danamon regions based on the division's needs and participants' domicile.

  • Yes, all trainees are provided with accommodation such as staying at Danamon's Corporate Campus during Phase 1, dormitory, 3x daily meals, snacks, and laundry.
